Northwick

Northwick is a small independent studio that builds focused, low-maintenance apps for Atlassian Cloud and publishes them on the Atlassian Marketplace. Our apps run on Atlassian Forge, keep customer data inside Atlassian's infrastructure, and do one job well without tracking, analytics, or clutter.

Apps

Review Due Confluence Cloud

Keep Confluence pages fresh. Set a review date and an owner on any page, see stale pages in a space dashboard, and get reminders when a review is due.

Acknowledge Confluence Cloud

Know who has read the page. Ask a Confluence group to confirm they have read a policy, a handbook or an SOP, see who has and who has not, and export the record — who confirmed which version of the page, and when — for an auditor. Weekly reminders are a comment on the page itself, and a removed confirmation is marked rather than erased.
